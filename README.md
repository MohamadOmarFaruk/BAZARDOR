## 📌 1. Project Name

**BazarDor (বাজার দর)**

A smart and user-friendly daily market price tracking web application built for the people of Bangladesh.

---

## 📝 2. Short Description

BazarDor is a Bangla-based daily market price tracking web application designed to help people in Bangladesh easily check the prices of essential commodities.

Users can explore products across different categories, compare current prices with historical records, and monitor daily price increases and decreases through a clean, responsive, and user-friendly interface.

The main goal of BazarDor is to make everyday market price information easier to access and help consumers stay informed about changes in commodity prices.

🌐 **Live Demo:** [Visit BazarDor](https://bazardor-c295.vercel.app/)

## ✨ 4. Five Key Features

### 📊 1. Daily Market Price Tracking
Explore the latest available prices of essential commodities, including:

- Rice and lentils
- Cooking oil
- Vegetables
- Fish and meat
- Eggs and spices

Get a convenient overview of everyday market prices in one place.

### 📈 2. Historical Price Comparison
Compare current commodity prices with previous records to understand market changes.

- Today's price
- Yesterday's price
- Last week's price
- Last month's price

### 🗂️ 3. Category-Based Filtering
Browse products through different categories and quickly find the commodities you're looking for without searching through the entire product collection.

### 🔥 4. Price Trend Monitoring
Stay informed about market price movements.

- Identify products with increased prices.
- Discover products with decreased prices.
- Monitor percentage changes in commodity prices.

### 🔐 5. User Authentication
A secure authentication system powered by **Better Auth**, providing:

- User registration
- User login
- Authentication management
